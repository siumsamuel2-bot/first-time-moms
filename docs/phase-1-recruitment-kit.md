## Contingency Planning

**If recruitment is slow:**
1. Increase Instagram ad budget from $500 to $750
2. Extend eligibility to mothers up to 9 months postpartum
3. Partner with postpartum doulas for referrals
4. Offer referral bonus: $25 extra for referring another participant

**If no-shows occur:**
1. Overbook by 2 participants (schedule 10 for 8 slots)
2. Keep 2 backup participants on standby
3. Offer evening/weekend slots for flexibility
4. Send reminder 1 hour before (not just 24 hours)
