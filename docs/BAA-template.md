# Business Associate Agreement (BAA) Template

This Business Associate Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between **Firstweeks Inc.** ("Covered Entity") and **[Cloud Provider Name]** ("Business Associate").

## 1. Definitions
- **PHI**: Protected Health Information as defined under HIPAA.
- **ePHI**: Electronic Protected Health Information.

## 2. Obligations of Business Associate
Business Associate agrees to:
- Use and disclose PHI only as permitted or required by this Agreement or as required by law.
- Implement appropriate safeguards to prevent the unauthorized use or disclosure of PHI.
- Report any unauthorized use or disclosure of PHI to the Covered Entity.
- Ensure that any subcontractors agree to the same restrictions and conditions.
- Make PHI available for amendment and incorporate any amendments.
- Make information available for accounting of disclosures.
- Comply with the Security Rule (45 CFR Part 160 and Subparts A and C of Part 164).

## 3. Permitted Uses and Disclosures
Business Associate may use or disclose PHI to:
- Provide services to the Covered Entity.
- Perform data aggregation for the Covered Entity's health care operations.
- Report abuse, neglect, or domestic violence.
- Respond to a subpoena, discovery request, or other lawful process.

## 4. Safeguards
Business Associate must:
- Implement administrative, physical, and technical safeguards that reasonably protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of ePHI.
- Encrypt ePHI at rest and in transit (AES-256, TLS 1.2+).
- Maintain audit logs of all access to ePHI.

## 5. Reporting and Mitigation
Business Associate must report any security incident, including any breach of unsecured PHI, to the Covered Entity within 24 hours of discovery.

## 6. Termination
This Agreement may be terminated by either party for material breach. Upon termination, Business Associate must return or destroy all PHI received from the Covered Entity.

## 7. Miscellaneous
- This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of **Delaware**.
- This Agreement is binding upon the parties and their respective successors and assigns.
