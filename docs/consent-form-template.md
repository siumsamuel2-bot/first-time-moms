## Introduction

You are invited to participate in a research study to test the Firstweeks app, a postpartum recovery support tool designed for first-time mothers. Your feedback will help improve the app for future users.

Please read this form carefully before deciding whether to participate. Ask questions if anything is unclear.

---

## Purpose of the Study

The purpose of this study is to evaluate the usability and user experience of the Firstweeks app. We want to ensure the app feels safe, supportive, and helpful for first-time mothers during the postpartum period.

---

## Procedures

If you agree to participate, you will:

1. Complete a 60-minute remote video session with a researcher
2. Review app wireframes and/or prototypes while sharing your screen
3. Share your thoughts and reactions to the designs
4. Answer questions about your postpartum experience
5. Complete a brief survey after the session

The session will be audio and video recorded for analysis purposes.

---

## Risks and Discomforts

**Minimal risks are associated with this study:**

- Some questions may touch on sensitive emotional topics related to postpartum mental health
- You may feel uncomfortable discussing certain experiences
- There is a small risk of breach of confidentiality (we take extensive precautions to prevent this)

**Support Resources:**
If you experience any distress during or after the session, please contact:

- **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline:** Call or text 988 (available 24/7, free, confidential)
- **Postpartum Support International:** 1-800-944-4773 | [postpartum.net](https://www.postpartum.net)
- **Crisis Text Line:** Text HOME to 741741

The research team is trained in mental health first aid and will pause or stop the session if you show signs of distress.

---

## Benefits

**Direct benefits to you:**
- $75 Amazon gift card (provided within 48 hours of session completion)
- Early access to the Firstweeks app (free for 6 months)
- Opportunity to shape postpartum care tools for future mothers

**Benefits to society:**
- Improved digital health tools for postpartum mothers

## Confidentiality

**Your privacy is our priority:**

- All data will be de-identified and stored using participant ID numbers (e.g., P01, P02)
- Video recordings will be stored on encrypted, password-protected drives
- Only the research team will have access to raw data
- Findings may be published or shared, but your identity will remain confidential
- Data will be retained for 3 years and then securely destroyed

**HIPAA Compliance:**
This study is conducted in compliance with HIPAA privacy rules. No protected health information will be collected or shared without your explicit consent.

---

## De-Identified Data Use

With your separate permission, your de-identified data may be used in the following ways:

**Aggregate Research Products:**
- De-identified, grouped data may be included in research publications, clinical studies, and quality improvement reports
- Only aggregated trends and statistics are shared — never individual responses

**No Individual-Level Data Sharing:**
- Your name, contact information, and all personally identifying details are removed before any data is used
- No individual-level data is shared with external partners, researchers, or institutions
- Your identity remains confidential in all published materials

**Your Right to Withdraw Consent:**
- You may withdraw consent for de-identified data use at any time, for any reason
- To withdraw, contact the research team at research@firstweeks.com or update your preferences in the app
- Withdrawal removes future data from the research pipeline; data already included in completed aggregate analyses cannot be retroactively removed
- Withdrawing consent does not affect your participation in the study or your access to the app

Selecting "No" to de-identified data use will not impact your ability to participate in the study or receive the $75 gift card.
