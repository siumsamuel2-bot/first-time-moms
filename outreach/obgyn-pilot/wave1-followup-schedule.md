## Response Scenarios

### Scenario A: Positive Response ("Interested, let's talk")
- **Action:** Schedule 20-minute intro call within 48 hours
- **Materials needed:** Pilot overview, calendar link, meeting prep guide
- **Goal:** Qualify fit and move to full demo

### Scenario B: Soft Interest ("Send me more info")
- **Action:** Send pilot overview PDF + case study + calendar link
- **Follow-up:** Schedule call for 1 week later
- **Goal:** Warm them up for a meeting

### Scenario C: Not Interested / Too Busy
- **Action:** Thank them, ask permission to follow up in 3 months
- **Note:** Flag for Wave 2 consideration
- **Goal:** Leave door open

### Scenario D: No Response
- **Action:** Follow cadence (Day 3 phone, Day 5 LinkedIn, Day 7 phone, Day 10 email)
- **Gate:** After Day 14, move to "No Response" and plan Wave 2
- **Goal:** Maximize touchpoints without being pushy
