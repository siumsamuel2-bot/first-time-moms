## Phone Script A — Practice Decision-Maker

### Opening (Receptionist Gatekeeper)

> Hi, this is [Name] from Firstweeks. I'm hoping to speak with your practice manager or medical director about a postpartum care partnership opportunity. Who would be the best person to connect with?

### When Connected to Decision-Maker

> Hi [Name], thanks for taking my call. I'll be brief — I'm reaching out because we're launching a postpartum digital health pilot with OB-GYN practices and I wanted to see if [Practice Name] might be interested.
>
> **Quick overview:** Firstweeks is a digital companion that checks in with new mothers twice a week between discharge and the 6-week visit. It catches complications like PPD, mastitis, and preeclampsia early — and gives your QI team de-identified outcome data to track your recovery metrics.
>
> **For your practice:** We cover all implementation costs. Your team commitment is about 4-6 hours a month. And you get real-time dashboards on your postpartum outcomes.
>
> Would you be open to a 20-minute exploratory call to see if this is a fit?

### Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We're too busy" | Totally understand. We designed this to be minimal lift — 4-6 hrs/month from a practice champion. Can I send you a one-pager and follow up next week? |
| "We already have a portal" | Portals are great for records, but Firstweeks is proactive clinical detection during the highest-risk window. Different tool for a different problem. |
| "We need compliance review" | Absolutely. I'll send our HIPAA summary and draft LOI. Happy to join a call with your compliance team. |
| "Not interested" | I appreciate your time. Would it be alright if I send our one-pager in case your team revisits postpartum QI initiatives? |
