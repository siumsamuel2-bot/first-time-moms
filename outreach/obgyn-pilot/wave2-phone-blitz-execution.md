## Revised Phone Scripts

### Gatekeeper Opening

> Hi, this is [Name] from Firstweeks. I'm reaching out because we're running a free postpartum monitoring pilot with select Chicago-area OB-GYN practices starting in July. Who would be the best person in your practice to speak with about postpartum care programs?

### Decision-Maker Pitch (< 60 sec)

> Hi [Name], thanks for taking my call. I know you're busy so I'll keep this brief.

> We built Firstweeks — a postpartum check-in system that goes between discharge and the 6-week visit. Mothers get 2 check-ins per week via text, <2 minutes each. Our AI identifies early warning signs of PPD, preeclampsia, hemorrhage, and mastitis — and alerts your team in real time.

> We're recruiting 2 practices for a free 8-week pilot starting July 21. Your practice gets a clinician dashboard, outcome data, and zero additional burden. We handle enrollment and support.

> Would you be open to a 20-minute call this week to see if this is a fit?

### Key Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We already do postpartum calls" | Great — our system supplements that. We catch trends your team might miss between touchpoints. Happy to share our clinical thresholds. |
| "No time" | I understand. The practice commitment is 4-6 hours/month total — we handle everything else. |
| "Send me info" | Happy to. What's the best email? I'll send a one-pager and follow up in 2 days. |
| "We'll think about it" | I appreciate that. Can I send you the LOI template so you can review the terms? No commitment — just to look over. |
| "Not interested" | Totally fair. Would it help to see a 5-minute demo of the clinician dashboard? |
