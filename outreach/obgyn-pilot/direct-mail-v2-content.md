## Mailer Contents

### Outer Package
**Format:** 9×12 rigid cardboard mailer with magnetic flap
**Exterior text:** "Firstweeks Postpartum Pilot — Enclosed: Practice Overview, Sample Dashboard, and Pilot Agreement"

### Insert 1: Cover Letter (8.5×11, folded)

**Printed on Firstweeks letterhead**

Dear Dr. [Last Name] / Practice Manager [Name],

This package contains everything you need to evaluate Firstweeks for your practice.

By now you may have seen our LinkedIn outreach or received a pilot overview by email. This letter is a more concrete invitation.

**We are selecting 2 OB-GYN practices for our July 7 pilot launch.** Your practice is on our shortlist because of your reputation for [academic affiliation / high delivery volume / quality innovation — personalize per practice].

**What's in it for your practice:**
- De-identified outcome dashboards for your QI team
- Early detection of PPD, mastitis, preeclampsia, and hemorrhage
- Zero-cost implementation, full support, 4-6 hrs/month commitment
- Co-authorship opportunity on our pilot outcomes paper (with your approval)

**The ask:** A 20-minute call to determine fit. If we both agree, we can move to LOI within the week.

I'll follow up by phone on [Jun 22-25] to discuss.

Sincerely,

[Your Name]
CMO, Firstweeks
[LinkedIn URL]
pilot@firstweeks.health

P.S. If I haven't heard from you by Jun 27, I'll take that as a pass for this wave — but I may reach out for future opportunities.
