# Provider-Sourced Warm Intro Channel — Practice Enrollment

**Purpose:** Third warm channel for [FIR-71](/FIR/issues/FIR-71) practice enrollment, alongside CEO intros (sent Jun 29) and ACOG chapters (done). The 29 sourced perinatal providers in `provider-candidate-tracker.md` hold referral relationships with OB-GYN practices in **all five priority metros**. A provider asking their affiliated practice to take a 20-minute call is the warmest available path that does not depend on the CEO's network.

**Companion docs:** `provider-candidate-tracker.md` (contact list), `response-conversion-playbook.md` (triage when replies arrive), `materials/pilot-overview.md`, `ceo-warm-intro-emails.md` (tone model)
**Owner:** CMO (copy) + human sender
**Version:** 1.0 — 2026-09-30
**Status:** Ready to launch — see §5 trigger

## 1. Why This Channel

- **Warm beats cold, proven twice:** Wave 1 cold digital = 0/25. The only warm paths ever executed (CEO intros Jun 29) are the current engine. Providers are a second warm layer the CEO's network doesn't cover.
- **Relationships already exist:** Perinatal therapists, IBCLCs, and pelvic floor PTs refer to OB-GYNs daily and often know practice medical directors by name.
- **Dual win:** every provider contacted is also a candidate for the app's provider network ([FIR-61](/FIR/issues/FIR-61)) — one conversation serves both tracks.
- **Metro match is complete:** all 5 priority practices have multiple sourced providers in their metro (§2).

**The ask is small:** one forwardable line to a practice contact. Providers are not being asked to sell, endorse outcomes, or commit anything.

## 3. Two-Step Ask Sequence

**Rule: value first, ask second.** Never lead with the intro request — the provider relationship is worth more than one intro.

### Step 1 — Network interest (the primary reason to contact)

Contact the provider about joining the Firstweeks provider network (this is [FIR-61](/FIR/issues/FIR-61)-aligned work that is due regardless):

> Hi [Name] — I'm the CMO at Firstweeks, a postpartum app for first-time moms. We're building a referral network of perinatal specialists ([credentials] caught my eye in [directory]) and our users in [metro] need exactly what you do. Referrals are warm intros only during pilot — no payment per referral, patients use their existing insurance/self-pay. Would you be open to a 15-minute call about joining? Details: [provider one-pager or `marketing/provider-onboarding-flow.md` link]

**If they reply at all**, log it (playbook §2 triage) and proceed to Step 2 in the *same* thread — never a cold second email.

### Step 2 — Practice intro request (only for engaged providers)

> One more thing while I have you — we're also running a free 6-month postpartum outcomes pilot with OB-GYN practices, and we're looking at [practice name] in [metro]. If that's a practice you refer to and you'd be comfortable making a one-line intro, it would genuinely help: "Firstweeks is running a postpartum outcomes pilot — worth 20 minutes." No pressure at all — joining the network is the main ask, and either way I'd love to get you set up.

**If they know a different practice better:** take it — any enrolled practice counts, not just the priority 5. Log the new target row in `pipeline-tracker.md`.

## 4. If the Provider Says Yes to the Intro

1. Ask for the introduction to be made by **reply-all adding us** (keeps the warmth transfer intact).
2. Send the forwardable blurb immediately so they can paste it (keep it one line — see §3 Step 2).
3. The practice's reply enters playbook §2 triage — same 4h SLA, same templates.
4. Log two rows in `pipeline-tracker.md`: provider contacted (Notes column of provider tracker) + new practice response (Response Tracking Log).
5. When the practice engages, the provider gets a thank-you note — protects the relationship for the app network too.

**Forwardable blurb (for the provider to paste):**

> Firstweeks is running a free 6-month postpartum outcomes pilot — 200 patients, de-identified QI dashboard for your team, zero cost, ~4 hours/month of staff time. Worth 20 minutes with their CMO?

## 5. Trigger & Rules

| Rule | Detail |

| **Launch trigger** | **Ready now.** Step 1 (network interest) is due [FIR-61](/FIR/issues/FIR-61) work independent of pilot status. If Wave 2 shows <1 meeting by **Oct 8**, Step 2 outreach accelerates to all 29 providers as the designated fallback channel. |

| **Volume discipline** | Max 10 provider contacts/day; these are relationships, not leads. |
| **No extraction** | A provider who declines the network ask is NOT asked for an intro. Value exchange must be real. |
| **Compliance** | No PHI, no patient names, no implication the provider endorses Firstweeks clinically. |
| **Escalation** | Any provider who names a practice contact → treat as Hot-equivalent, playbook §3.1 reply, intro same day. |
