## Why ACOG Chapters

State chapters of the American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists (ACOG) reach OB-GYN decision-makers in a trusted, peer-reviewed context. A recommendation from a chapter is worth 10x a cold email.

Chapters offer three entry points:
1. **Newsletter / digital bulletin** — brief pilot description + link to learn more
2. **Chapter meeting presentation** — 10-15 min slot at annual or quarterly meeting
3. **Email distribution to membership** — chapter blast to all OB-GYNs in the state

## 400-Word Clinical Article (Submission-Ready)

**Title:** Closing the Postpartum Monitoring Gap: A Digital Bridge from Discharge to the 6-Week Visit

**Subtitle:** How adaptive AI check-ins can reduce readmissions and improve maternal outcomes

The six weeks following childbirth carry the highest risk of maternal morbidity and mortality in the entire perinatal period (ACOG, 2018). Yet the standard of care leaves a 42-day gap between hospital discharge and the postpartum visit — with no structured monitoring during the window when PPD, mastitis, preeclampsia, and hemorrhage most commonly surface.

**The clinical challenge:**
- 1 in 8 women experience postpartum depression; up to 50% go undiagnosed (Ko et al., 2017)
- Postpartum readmissions average $8,659 per admission, with infection and hemorrhage as leading causes (Mohan et al., 2022)
- Racial disparities persist: Black and Latina mothers experience severe maternal morbidity at 2-3x the rate of white mothers (CDC, 2023)
- PPD peaks at weeks 3-4 postpartum, before the standard 6-week follow-up (Wisner et al., 2013)
- C-section infections most often present between days 7-14 — outside any scheduled care window

**The approach:** Adaptive digital check-ins that tailor questions to each patient's delivery type, recovery trajectory, and reported symptoms — then escalate based on validated clinical thresholds. This mirrors the monitoring cadence used successfully in telehealth hypertension and diabetes programs, adapted specifically for postpartum care.

**How Firstweeks delivers:**
1. **Symptom screening:** 3-5 questions, 2x/week, adapted to delivery type and evolving symptoms
2. **Clinical detection:** AI identifies early warning patterns for PPD, preeclampsia, hemorrhage, mastitis, and wound infection against validated thresholds
3. **Escalation:** Care-team alerts when symptoms cross clinical boundaries, with contextual resources routed to the patient
4. **QI data:** De-identified cohort dashboards enabling practices to benchmark outcomes, track readmission rates, and identify disparity gaps

**Pilot opportunity:** Firstweeks is enrolling 2 OB-GYN practices in an 8-week pilot starting July 7. Zero cost to practices. The practice champions commit ~4-6 hrs/month. Full onboarding and implementation support included.

To learn more or express interest, contact [pilot@firstweeks.health](mailto:pilot@firstweeks.health).

**References:** ACOG Committee Opinion No. 736 (2018); Ko et al., *Obstet Gynecol* (2017); Mohan et al., *J Womens Health* (2022); Wisner et al., *JAMA Psychiatry* (2013); CDC Pregnancy Mortality Surveillance System (2023).
