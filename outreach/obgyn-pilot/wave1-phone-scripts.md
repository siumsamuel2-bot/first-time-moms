## Script

### Opening

> Hi, this is [Your Name] from Firstweeks. I hope I’m catching you at a good time. I’m reaching out on behalf of our postpartum digital health pilot. Do you have two minutes for a quick overview?

### Pitch (if they have time)

> We’re launching a clinical validation pilot with 2 OB-GYN practices. Firstweeks is a digital postpartum companion that checks in with new mothers twice a week in the first 6 weeks after delivery—when the risk of complications like PPD, hemorrhage, and infection is highest.
>
> We provide:
> - AI-powered symptom check-ins (3–5 min, twice/week)
> - Real-time alerts to your care team when thresholds are crossed
> - De-identified outcome dashboards for your QI team
> - A white-glove setup and clinical success manager
>
> **What we need from you:** A practice champion (4–6 hrs/month), in-clinic recruitment support, and a BAA for de-identified data sharing. The pilot lasts 6 months, and we cover all costs. We’d love to include [Practice Name] as one of our 2 pilot partners.

### Objection Handling

- **“We already have a patient portal.”**
  > Portals are great for records, but Firstweeks is specifically designed for the postpartum window—proactive clinical detection rather than reactive access to records.

- **“We’re too busy right now.”**
  > I completely understand. We designed this to require minimal time from your staff. Can I send a one-page overview and follow up next week?

- **“We need to check with our compliance team.”**
  > Absolutely. I’ll send over our HIPAA summary and a draft LOI. Would it help if I joined a brief call with you and your compliance officer?

### Close / Next Step

> Would you be open to a 20-minute exploratory call this week or next? I’ll send over our pilot overview and a calendar link.
