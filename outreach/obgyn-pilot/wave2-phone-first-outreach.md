# Wave 2 — Phone-First Outreach Plan

**Trigger:** Wave 1 fails to close 2+ LOIs by Jun 27
**Owner:** CMO
**Timeline:** Jun 28 – Jul 14
**Goal:** 2+ pilot LOIs signed by Jul 14 (one-week slip from original Jul 7)

---

## Lessons from Wave 1

| What Failed | Why | Wave 2 Fix |
|-------------|-----|------------|
| LinkedIn InMail (25 sent, 0 responses) | Healthcare decision-makers don't check LinkedIn for vendor pitches | Skip LinkedIn entirely |
| Email follow-up (25 sent, 0 responses) | Email goes to admin/trash without warm intro | Email only AFTER phone contact |
| Direct mail (8 sent, 0 responses) | Plain envelope didn't stand out | Branded mailer v2 (see direct-mail-v2-content.md) |
| National geography (25 practices across 15 states) | Too diluted for relationship-building | Concentrate on 1-2 metro regions |
| 21-day outreach window | Too slow for healthcare | 7-day sprint per wave |

### 4. Revised Script — Phone-First

**Opening (Gatekeeper):**

> Hi, this is [Name] from Firstweeks. I'm reaching out to your practice manager about a postpartum care improvement program we're piloting with [number] practices in Chicago. Who would be the best person to connect with?

**When Connected to Decision-Maker:**

> Hi [Name], thanks for taking my call. I know you're busy, so I'll be direct.
>
> We're running a postpartum monitoring pilot starting July 21 and your practice is one of a handful we've identified in the Chicago area that would be an ideal fit.
>
> **Here's the short version:** Firstweeks does twice-weekly check-ins with new moms between discharge and the 6-week visit. We catch PPD, mastitis, preeclampsia, and hemorrhage early — and give your team real-time outcome dashboards.
>
> It costs your practice nothing. We handle implementation. Your team commitment is 4-6 hours a month.
>
> Would you be open to a 20-minute call this week to see if this makes sense for [Practice Name]?

### 5. Objection Handling — Wave 2 Specific

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We didn't respond to your email/LinkedIn" | I understand — digital is noisy. That's why I'm calling. Can I send you a fresh one-pager and follow up in a few days? |
| "We're already piloting something" | Great — what are you piloting? We may complement, not compete. Happy to share our clinical specs for comparison. |
| "We need to see IRB approval" | We're operating under BAA, not IRB, for this pilot. Happy to walk your compliance team through our HIPAA framework. |
| "Why should we trust a startup?" | Fair question. We're working with [CEO's network practices if intro is made]. Happy to connect you with our clinical advisory board. |
