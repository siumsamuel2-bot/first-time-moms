# Meeting Prep Guide: OB-GYN Practice Intro Call

**Purpose:** Standardize the 20-minute intro call to qualify pilot fit and build toward a full demo.

---

## Pre-Meeting Checklist

- [ ] Review practice profile (size, location, affiliations)
- [ ] Open pipeline tracker; note status
- [ ] Have pilot overview PDF ready to share screen
- [ ] Confirm calendar holds backup slots if call runs long

---

## Call Agenda (20 Minutes)

### 1. Opening / Rapport (2 min)
> "Thank you for taking time today. I reached out because [Practice Name] stood out for [specific detail]. We're looking for 2 practices to join a clinical validation pilot, and your profile is a strong match."

### 2. Context Setting (3 min)
> "Postpartum complications — PPD, hemorrhage, infection, DVT — peak in the first 6 weeks after delivery, but standard care often misses the window. Firstweeks is a digital postpartum companion that proactively checks in with new mothers twice a week."

### 3. Pilot Overview (5 min)
- AI-powered symptom check-ins (3–5 min, 2×/week)
- Real-time clinical alerts to care team
- De-identified outcome dashboards for QI
- White-glove setup + clinical success manager
- 6-month pilot, zero cost to practice

### 4. Qualification Questions (5 min)
1. "How do you currently monitor postpartum patients between the 2-week and 6-week visits?"
2. "Have you explored digital health tools for postpartum care before?"
3. "Who would be the right internal champion for a pilot like this?"
4. "What's your typical process for evaluating new clinical tools?"
5. "Would de-identified outcome data and QI dashboards be useful for your team?"

### 5. Next Steps / Close (3 min)
- Confirm interest level (High / Medium / Low)
- Offer full demo (30 min, includes CTO or clinical lead)
- Send LOI draft if interest is High
- Schedule follow-up date

### 6. Notes & Action Items (2 min)
- Document key concerns
- Identify blockers (compliance, budget, stakeholder buy-in)
- Set follow-up date

---

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We already have a patient portal." | "Portals handle records; Firstweeks is clinical detection for the postpartum window. Complementary, not competitive." |
| "We don't have bandwidth." | "Designed for minimal staff time — 4–6 hrs total over 6 months. We handle patient onboarding and support." |
| "Compliance needs to review." | "We'll provide HIPAA summary + draft LOI. Happy to join a call with your compliance officer." |
| "We want to see data first." | "Pilot partners get monthly dashboards + readmission analysis. We also have anonymized pilot metrics from our development phase." |
| "Cost?" | "The pilot is fully funded by Firstweeks. No cost to the practice." |

---

## Post-Meeting Actions

- [ ] Update pipeline tracker with outcome
- [ ] Send thank-you email within 24 hours
- [ ] Share any requested materials (pilot overview, HIPAA summary, LOI)
- [ ] Schedule next step (demo, compliance call, LOI review)
- [ ] Flag for CEO if practice is high-priority
