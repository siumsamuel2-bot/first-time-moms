# Firstweeks Pilot Program — One-Page Overview

## Improve Postpartum Outcomes. Capture Real-Time Data. Reduce Readmissions.

**Firstweeks** is a digital postpartum companion that identifies physical and mental health complications in the first six weeks after delivery — the period with the highest maternal morbidity and mortality risk.

---

### The Problem
- 1 in 8 women experience postpartum depression; up to half go undiagnosed.
- Postpartum readmissions cost an average $8,659 per admission.
- Black and Latina mothers experience complications at 2–3× the rate of white mothers.
- Clinical net-promoter scores (NPS) for postpartum care remain low due to no structured follow-up.

### Firstweeks Solution
- AI-powered check-ins — 3–5 questions, 2×/week, adapted to daily symptoms.
- Clinical-grade detection of PPD, mastitis, thrombosis, preeclampsia, hemorrhage, and more.
- Care-team alerts and resource routing when thresholds are crossed.
- De-identified outcome data for continuous quality improvement.

### Pilot Scope (2 OB-GYN Practices)
- **Enrollment goal:** 200 postpartum patients per practice over 4 months.
- **Duration:** 6 months (2-month ramp-up + 4-month data collection).
- **Patient touchpoints:** Symptom monitoring, educational content, mood tracking, lactation support.
- **Clinical deliverables:** Weekly cohort dashboards, monthly readmission analysis, QI recommendations.
- **Data ownership:** Practice retains identifiable patient data; Firstweeks receives de-identified outcomes under BAA.

### What We Need From You
- Practice manager or medical director as pilot champion (4–6 hrs/month).
- Patient recruitment support (in-clrollment, flyers, informed consent process).
- Access to EHR for de-identified data extraction (under BAA).
- Monthly check-in with Firstweeks clinical success team.

### What the Patient Sees
1. **Day 3 postpartum:** Text invites patient to create account in Firstweeks app.
2. **Days 4–42:** Twice-weekly check-ins via push notification.
3. **Symptom alert (if triggered):** Care team receives dashboard alert + patient gets contextual education and care resources.
4. **Week 6:** Discharge from Firstweeks; exit survey and NPS collected.
5. **Ongoing:** Postpartum education library and community remain available.
