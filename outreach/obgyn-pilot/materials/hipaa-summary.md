# HIPAA Summary for Firstweeks Pilot Partnership

## Data Practices

### What Data We Collect
- **Patient-reported outcomes:** Symptom check-ins, mood scores (EPDS-lite), engagement metrics, lactation journaling.
- **Clinical data (practice-provided, de-identified):** Delivery date, mode of delivery, APGAR, gestational age, select EHR fields (under BAA).

### How We Protect It
- **Encryption in transit and at rest** (AES-256).
- **Access controls:** Role-based access; no individual patient data leaves the platform.
- **Audit logging:** All data access logged and reviewable.
- **Business Associate Agreement (BAA):** Executed before pilot begins; governs all data sharing.
- **No sale of patient data to third parties.**
- **Patient consent:** To participate in the pilot, patients must provide informed consent through the app.
- **Minimum necessary standard:** We only request the clinical data required for analytical outcomes and safety alerts.
- **Data retention:** Patient data can be deleted at any time upon request; pilot data is retained for analysis per the BAA term.

### What Happens to De-identified Data
- Firstweeks aggregates de-identified data for the practice-level QI report.
- Aggregated outcomes may be used by Firstweeks for product improvement, case studies, and academic publications (no individual patients identified).
- Institutional licensing (separate agreement) may allow for broader aggregation across practices.

### Patient Rights
- Right to access their data.
- Right to request deletion.
- Right to opt out of the pilot at any time (care remains unchanged).
- Right to lodge a complaint with HHS or state attorney general.
