# Direct Mail v2 — Follow-up Phone Script

**Window:** Jul 1-3, 2026 (after mail delivery expected Jun 29-30)
**Goal:** Confirm receipt, gauge interest, schedule exploratory call

---

## Pre-Call Preparation

- [ ] Confirm package delivered via FedEx tracking
- [ ] Review practice notes from LinkedIn/email history
- [ ] Have Calendly link ready to book call
- [ ] Know the decision-maker name

---

## Script

### Introduction (30 sec)

> "Hello [Name], this is [Your Name] from Firstweeks. I'm calling to follow up on the package we sent last week — the branded mailer with the pilot overview and sample dashboard. Did you receive it?"

**[If yes:]** "Great — do you have it handy or would you like me to give you a quick summary?"

**[If no:]** "No problem — let me check the tracking. It should arrive by [date]. I'll follow up again after it lands, or I can send a digital version in the meantime."

---

### Pitch (1-2 min)

> "To give you the 60-second version: Firstweeks is a postpartum digital companion that helps your patients check in 2x per week on symptoms like PPD, mastitis, and preeclampsia. When the AI detects a concern, your care team gets an alert. Your practice gets a weekly dashboard with readmission data, complication trends, and QI insights.
>
> We're selecting 2 practices for a July 7 pilot launch. Zero cost to you — just 4-6 hours per month from a practice champion."

---

### Qualification Questions

> "A few quick questions to see if this could be a fit:"
>
> 1. "How many postpartum patients do you see per month?"
> 2. "Do you currently have any structured postpartum follow-up between the 2-week and 6-week visit?"
> 3. "Has readmission rate or postpartum NPS come up as a priority for your QI team?"

---

### Close (30 sec)

> "Based on what you've shared, I think this could be a strong fit. Would you be open to a 20-minute exploratory call next week? I can send you a Calendly link right now."

**[If yes:]** Send Calendly link immediately. Follow up with email confirmation.

**[If need to discuss internally:]** "Totally understand. When would be a good time to check back? And is there anyone else on your team I should send materials to?"

**[If not interested:]**
> "I appreciate your time. If anything changes or you'd like to learn more down the road, please feel free to reach out at pilot@firstweeks.health."
