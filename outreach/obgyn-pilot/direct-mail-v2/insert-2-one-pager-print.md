### Improve Postpartum Outcomes. Capture Real-Time Data. Reduce Readmissions.

**Firstweeks** is a digital postpartum companion that identifies physical and mental health complications in the first six weeks after delivery — the period with the highest maternal morbidity and mortality risk.

---

#### The Problem We Solve

| Statistic | Impact |
|-----------|--------|
| 1 in 8 women experience PPD | Up to half go undiagnosed |
| Postpartum readmissions avg $8,659 | 15-20% are preventable |
| Black & Latina mothers: 2-3x complication rate | Disparity starts in the "4th trimester" |
| Low clinical NPS for postpartum care | No structured follow-up between visits |

#### How Firstweeks Works

> **Patient** → 3-5 min check-ins, 2x/week via app → **AI risk detection** → **Care team alerts** → **De-identified outcomes dashboard**

---

#### Pilot at a Glance

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Scope | 2 OB-GYN practices |
| Enrollment | ~200 postpartum patients per practice |
| Duration | 6 months (2-mo ramp + 4-mo data collection) |
| Patient touchpoints | Symptom monitoring, mood tracking, education, lactation support |
| Practice deliverables | Weekly cohort dashboards, monthly readmission analysis, QI recommendations |
| Data model | BAA-backed: practice owns identifiable data, Firstweeks receives de-identified outcomes |

#### What Your Practice Gets

| Benefit | Impact |
|---------|--------|
| Patient retention | 24/7 digital engagement between visits |
| Risk detection | Early identification of PPD, mastitis, preeclampsia, hemorrhage, DVT |
| Readmission reduction | Target: 15-20% decrease in 30-day readmissions |
| NPS improvement | Structured follow-up builds patient trust |
| Research data | De-identified outcome datasets for QI & publication |
| Revenue potential | Future digital care service fees (TBD with payers) |

#### What We Need From You

- **Pilot champion** (practice manager or medical director — 4-6 hrs/month)
- **Patient recruitment support** (in-clinic enrollment, flyers, informed consent)
- **EHR data access** (de-identified extraction under BAA)
- **Monthly check-in** with Firstweeks clinical success team
