## 1. Brand Positioning

**One-liner:**  
Firstweeks is the personalized postpartum companion that catches what new moms miss — so they can focus on their baby, not their anxiety.

**Positioning statement:**  
For first-time mothers who feel overwhelmed in the weeks after childbirth, Firstweeks is a mobile app that uses AI to track recovery milestones, flag early warning signs, and connect them to a community of women who get it. Unlike generic parenting apps, we combine clinical-grade screening with personalized daily guidance — turning the "fourth trimester" from a blur of worry into a supported, informed journey.

**Brand promise:**  
You're not alone. We've got the science, the community, and the daily check-in to make your postpartum weeks feel manageable.

**Brand personality:**  
Warm, authoritative, non-judgmental. Like the friend who's also a doctor.

## 2. Audience & Personas

### Primary: First-time moms (B2C)
- **Age:** 25–38
- **Stage:** 0–12 weeks postpartum
- **Mindset:** Sleep-deprived, Googling symptoms at 2 AM, wants reassurance without being dismissed
- **Needs:** Daily guidance, red-flag detection, community validation
- **Fears:** "Am I doing this right?", "Is this pain normal?", "I don't want to bother my doctor"
- **Channel preference:** Instagram, private Facebook groups, WhatsApp mom circles, OB-GYN referral

### Secondary: Clinicians & practice managers (B2B)
- **Role:** OB-GYN, midwife, practice administrator
- **Need:** Reduce postpartum ER bounce-backs, improve HEDIS scores, offer patients a retention tool
- **Pain:** Can't monitor patients between visits, limited bandwidth for callbacks
- **Buying criteria:** Clinical validation, HIPAA compliance, patient engagement data

### Institutional: Research & payor
- **Role:** Population health director, clinical researcher
- **Need:** De-identified outcome data, readmission reduction metrics
- **Decision driver:** Peer-reviewed validation, data quality, IRB clearance

## 3. Voice Pillars

| Pillar | What it means | Example |
|--------|--------------|---------|
| **Clinically kind** | Evidence-backed but never cold. Cite sources without sounding like a textbook. | "Studies show that 1 in 7 new moms experience postpartum anxiety. Here's what to watch for — and what actually helps." |
| **Calmly confident** | You're in good hands. We've done the research so you don't have to. | "We built our symptom tracker with OB-GYNs. If something's off, you'll know — without the panic." |
| **Permission-giving** | It's okay to not be okay. You don't have to love every moment. | "Some days you just need to survive. That counts too." |
| **Straightforward** | No fluff. No wellness-washing. Busy moms don't have time for marketing copy. | "Open the app. Answer 3 questions. Get your plan for today." |

## 4. Do / Say / Don't Rules

### Do:
- Use "postpartum" over "postnatal" (broader recognition, less clinical)
- Lead with empathy before data
- Normalize struggle — "Many new moms feel this way"
- Use second-person ("you") in all consumer-facing copy
- Keep sentences under 20 words where possible
- Use contractions (you're, don't, we'll)

### Say:
- "Your fourth trimester" (not "postpartum period")
- "Recovery milestones" (not "medical checkpoints")
- "Daily check-in" (not "symptom assessment")
- "Catch complications early" (not "diagnose conditions")
- "Mom community" (not "user network")
- "Your personalized plan" (not "algorithmic output")
- "AI-powered insights" (not "machine learning")

### Don't:
- Don't use scare tactics ("This could kill you")
- Don't promise medical diagnosis (we flag, not diagnose)
- Don't minimize real pain ("Just get some rest")
- Don't use jargon without a plain-English translation
- Don't compare moms ("Other women bounce back by week 4")
- Don't use "breastfeeding" as default — "feeding your baby" covers all paths

## 5. Vocabulary

| Use this | Not this |
|----------|----------|
| Birth parent / new mom | Mother (unless user self-identifies) |
| Postpartum | Postnatal |
| Check-in | Assessment / evaluation |
| Insights | Analytics / reporting |
| Your care team | Your healthcare provider |
| Flag / alert | Warning / alarm |
| Community | Support group (too clinical) |
| Milestones | Targets |
| Guide | Program / curriculum |
| Track your recovery | Monitor your health |
| Personalized | Customized (feels less clinical) |

## 7. Tone by Channel

| Channel | Tone | Notes |
|---------|------|-------|
| App UI | Warm, minimal, instructional | Every screen should feel like a helpful nudge, not a dashboard |
| Email (onboarding) | Welcoming, reassuring | 5-email drip: welcome → first check-in → community → provider → upgrade |
| Email (institutional) | Professional, data-forward | Case studies, white papers, pilot results |
| Social (Instagram) | Relatable, visual, short-form | Reels of real mom moments, tips, "you're not alone" stories |
| Social (FB groups / Reddit) | Conversational, peer-focused | Community manager replies as "the Firstweeks team" — never corporate |
| Direct mail | Personal, warm, tangible | Hand-signed feel, letter-grade paper |

---

## 8. Brand Visual Keywords

- **Colors:** Warm blush, calming sage, clean white, charcoal text
- **Imagery:** Real postpartum bodies (stretch marks, nursing, tired eyes, laughing), not stock perfection
- **Typography:** Rounded sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Nunito) for digital; serif for print (e.g., Source Serif)
- **Photography style:** Natural light, candid, inclusive (all body types, feeding methods, family structures)
