## 3. Screening Call Guide (15 minutes)

### Call Flow

| Time | Section | Content |
|------|---------|---------|
| 0–2 min | **Intro** | Thank them for their time. Brief Firstweeks pitch (2 sentences). Confirm they received the outreach email. |
| 2–5 min | **Practice overview** | Ask about their practice: patient volume, postpartum % of caseload, typical response time for new referrals |
| 5–8 min | **Logistics** | Telehealth → what platform, insurance panels accepted, new patient availability window |
| 8–10 min | **Interest & fit** | What appeals to them about the partnership? Any concerns? |
| 10–12 min | **Next steps** | If strong fit → invite to onboarding. Describe what onboarding involves (profile setup, referral flow test). |
| 12–15 min | **Q&A / Close** | Answer questions. Confirm best email for follow-up. Thank them. |

### Scoring During Call

Fill in rubric factors 2, 4, 5 based on conversation:

- **Availability (×2):** 0 = >2 weeks / unclear, 1 = 1–2 weeks, 2 = within 1 week
- **Insurance (×2):** 0 = self-pay only, 1 = some plans, 2 = major commercial insurance
- **Responsiveness (×1):** 0 = slow/unclear, 1 = responds within a week, 2 = within 48hr or same day

### Auto-Qualification Rules

- **Auto-accept** if total score ≥ 18 AND postpartum experience score ≥ 4
- **Auto-reject** if postpartum experience = 0 OR total < 12
- **Discuss with CTO** if candidate is strong but has technical concerns about referral flow

## 5. Selection Criteria Summary

15 candidates (5 per specialty) will proceed to onboarding [FIR-61](/FIR/issues/FIR-61).

- **Minimum score:** 12/25
- **Mandatory:** Postpartum experience score ≥ 4 (must have dedicated postpartum practice)
- **Tiebreaker:** Availability within 1 week > Insurance acceptance > Referral responsiveness
- **Geography balance:** At least 2 per metro across the 2 confirmed pilot geographies
- **Attrition buffer:** Reserves maintained for quick replacement if any selected candidate drops out during onboarding
