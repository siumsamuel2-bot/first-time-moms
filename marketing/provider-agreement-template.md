## PROVIDER AGREEMENT

**Between:** Firstweeks, Inc. ("Firstweeks") and **[Provider Name]** ("Provider")

**Date:** **[Date]**

### 1. Services

Provider agrees to accept warm referrals from the Firstweeks postpartum monitoring platform during the pilot period. Referrals consist of postpartum patients whose daily check-ins have crossed an escalation threshold and elected to speak with a screened provider.

### 2. Provider Obligations

- Respond to referral requests within 1 business day via the provider dashboard
- Maintain active license and insurance coverage throughout the pilot term
- Provide telehealth or in-person services based on availability indicated in the provider profile
- Ensure all patient communications comply with HIPAA and applicable privacy laws

### 3. Firstweeks Obligations

- Provide provider with dashboard access to manage incoming referrals
- Deliver patient context: symptom summary and reason for referral (no PHI until accepted)
- Notify provider when a new pilot practice is active in their geography

### 4. Compensation

**Pilot phase:** Referrals are warm intros only — no payment per referral. Patients pay for services directly using their existing insurance or self-pay fee schedule.

**Post-pilot:** Firstweeks and Provider will negotiate a revenue-sharing or per-referral model in good faith based on pilot volume and outcomes.

### 5. Data & Privacy

- Provider receives only the information necessary to determine fit and respond to a referral
- Full patient PHI is exchanged only after the patient accepts the provider introduction
- Provider agrees not to use Firstweeks platform data for any purpose beyond patient care

### 6. Term & Termination

- **Term:** Duration of the pilot (8 weeks from first referral), auto-renewing monthly
- **Termination:** Either party may terminate with 7 days written notice
- Firstweeks may immediately terminate if Provider fails to respond to referrals within 1 business day for 3 consecutive requests

### 7. Relationship

Provider is an independent contractor, not an employee of Firstweeks. Nothing in this agreement creates a partnership or agency relationship.

### 8. Governing Law

This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
