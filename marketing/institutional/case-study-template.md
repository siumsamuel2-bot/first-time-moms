## Abstract (150 words)

> **Background:** The standard 6-week postpartum visit captures a single snapshot, missing daily symptom trends that precede complications.
>
> **Methods:** [Practice Name] enrolled [N] postpartum patients in a 8-week digital daily check-in program using Firstweeks. Patients completed 3-5 adapted questions twice weekly covering sleep, mood, physical recovery, feeding, and escalation symptoms.
>
> **Results:** [Key finding 1, e.g., "42% of patients reported a mental health symptom that would have gone undetected until 6-week visit"]; [Key finding 2, e.g., "readmission rate in monitored cohort was X% vs Y% baseline"]; [Key finding 3, e.g., "NPS score of Z vs practice baseline of W"].
>
> **Conclusion:** Daily digital postpartum monitoring enables earlier detection of complications and provides structured outcome data for quality improvement.

## Methods (300 words)

### Pilot Design
- 8-week digital daily check-in program
- Patient recruitment: in-clinic enrollment at discharge
- Inclusion/exclusion criteria
- Technology: Firstweeks mobile app, HIPAA-compliant

### Data Collection
- Daily check-in domains: sleep, mood, feeding, physical recovery, escalation symptoms
- Survey frequency: 2x/week, adapted based on symptom trajectory
- Escalation protocol: automated alerts to care team when thresholds crossed
