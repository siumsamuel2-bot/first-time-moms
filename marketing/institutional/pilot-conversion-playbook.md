# Pilot Practice Conversion Playbook

**Goal:** Convert 1+ pilot OB-GYN practices into paying institutional data customers  
**Timeline:** Pilot midpoint (Week 4) → Pilot completion (Week 8) → Post-pilot (Sep+)  
**Owner:** CMO

---

## The Conversion Funnel

```
Pilot Practice Enrolled → Quarterly Reports (Free) → See Value → 
→ Case Study Published → Upsell to Standard/Professional Tier → 
→ Reference for 5 More Networks
```

---

## Phase 1: Value Demonstration (Weeks 1-4)

### Setup
- At pilot kickoff (Jul 7), confirm practice's top 3 research/quality questions
  - "What postpartum outcomes matter most to your QI committee?"
  - "Is there a specific complication rate you're trying to reduce?"
  - "What data would make your quarterly board report stronger?"

### Week 2 Check-in
- Share early aggregate trends (n≥5 only)
  - "Your cohort's average sleep deficit is 3.1h — here's how it compares to the other pilot site"
- Ask: "What patterns stand out to you? What would you like to drill into?"

### Week 4 (Midpoint) Readout
- **Schedule 30-min virtual readout** with pilot champion + practice leadership
- **Agenda:**
  1. Cohort overview (demographics, engagement)
  2. Key findings (sleep, mood, feeding trends)
  3. Escalation summary (how many, what types, outcomes)
  4. Patient testimonials (if opt-in consent obtained)
  5. Preview: "This data could power your quarterly QI reports"
- **Deliverable:** Midpoint outcome report (PDF, 5-7 pages)
- **Gauge interest:** "Would a quarterly version of this report be useful for your QI program?"

---

## Phase 2: Case Study & Relationship Deepening (Weeks 5-7)

### Week 5: Case Study Co-Authoring
- Propose co-authored case study for OB-GYN trade publication
  - "Postpartum Recovery Outcomes with Daily Digital Monitoring: A Pilot at [Practice Name]"
- Practice gets authorship credit + publication visibility
- Firstweeks gets validated case study for sales collateral

### Week 6: Value Reinforcement
- Share personalized practice analytics:
  - Patients flagged early vs. if no monitoring existed
  - Estimated readmission avoidance (based on escalation acceptance)
  - Patient satisfaction data (NPS from pilot cohort)
- **Framing:** "This is what you'd get quarterly with a Standard data subscription"

### Week 7: Pricing Introduction
- Have informal conversation:
  - "We're planning to offer ongoing data access to pilot partners at a special rate"
  - "What budget would your QI department have for something like this?"
  - "Who would need to approve a $X/year data subscription?"

---

## Phase 3: Close (Week 8+)

### Week 8 (Pilot End) Readout
- **Schedule 45-min readout** with practice leadership + QI director
- **Agenda:**
  1. Full 8-week outcomes report
  2. ROI analysis (early detection, patient satisfaction, workflow impact)
  3. Testimonials from patients and care team
  4. Case study draft review
  5. **Pitch:** "Here's what ongoing access looks like"

### Pricing for Pilot Practice Conversion

| Tier | Annual Price | Intro Offer (Year 1) | What They Get |
|------|-------------|---------------------|---------------|
| **Basic** | $6,000/yr | $3,000/yr | Quarterly aggregate reports + clinician dashboard |
| **Professional** | $24,000/yr | $12,000/yr (first 6mo) | Monthly updates + custom queries + raw cohort exports |
| **Enterprise** | $60,000/yr | $36,000/yr | Real-time dashboard + API access + dedicated support |

**Special pilot partner pricing:** 50% off Year 1 (locked for 3 years if signed by Oct 1, 2026)

### Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We don't have budget for this" | "Year 1 is $3,000 — that's less than the cost of one preventable readmission. And you've already seen the data in action this pilot." |
| "We need to see more data first" | "You've got 8 weeks of your own patients' data. What additional data would help you make the decision?" |
| "Our QI director would need to approve" | "Can you introduce me? I'd be happy to present the value to them." |
| "We already track outcomes" | "What's your current tracking method? Our daily check-in captures trends between visits that episodic data misses." |
| "This is interesting but not a priority" | "I understand. Would you be open to a quarterly check-in as your data grows? We can revisit when QI goals shift." |

## Post-Conversion: Reference Program

Once a practice converts to paid:
- Request permission to use as reference for 5 other networks
- Offer $1,000 annual discount for each successful referral that closes
- Feature practice in blog post and case study
- Invite practice champion to speak at upcoming conference (paid travel)
