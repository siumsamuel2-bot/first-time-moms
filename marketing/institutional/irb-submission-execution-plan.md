## What IRB Will Receive

The submission requests exempt determination under 45 CFR 46.104(d)(4) for:
- De-identified postpartum recovery data collected via Firstweeks app
- No PHI collected (HIPAA Safe Harbor de-identification)
- Users consent to data use at onboarding
- Data used for aggregated outcome dashboards and institutional licensing
