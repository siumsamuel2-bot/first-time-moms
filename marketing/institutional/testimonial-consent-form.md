**Purpose:** Obtain patient consent to use de-identified quotes and outcome data in Firstweeks marketing materials (case studies, blog posts, social media, investor materials, and institutional sales collateral).

**IRB Status:** Exempt per WCG IRB pathway (see [irb-strategy-memo.md](../irb-strategy-memo.md))

## Consent Form

### Firstweeks Patient Story & Outcome Data Consent

**Study Title:** Firstweeks Postpartum Recovery Pilot Program
**Practice:** [Practice Name]
**IRB:** [IRB reference number, if applicable]

#### What This Is About
You participated in the Firstweeks postpartum recovery program through [Practice Name]. We'd like your permission to share your experience and de-identified outcome data to help other mothers and improve postpartum care.

#### What We're Asking
We'd like to use:
1. **Your de-identified recovery data:** Your daily check-in responses, recovery milestones, and outcomes. All data will be anonymized — no name, date of birth, address, or other identifying information will be included.
2. **Your quotes:** Selected quotes from your experience with the program, if you'd like to share them.
3. **Your photo (optional):** A photograph (with face visible or obscured, per your preference).

#### How Your Information Will Be Used
- Case studies about postpartum recovery outcomes
- Blog posts and educational content for new mothers
- Marketing materials for healthcare providers
- Presentations at medical conferences
- Grant applications and research publications

#### What Will NOT Be Shared
- Your name (a pseudonym or first name only, per your preference)
- Your exact date of birth or delivery date
- Your address or exact location
- Your medical record number
- Any information that could identify you to others

#### Your Rights
- **You are not required to participate.** Your care at [Practice Name] will not be affected whether or not you consent.
- **You can change your mind.** You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting pilot@firstweeks.health. We will stop using your information in future materials, though materials already published cannot be recalled.
- **You can choose what to share.** You may consent to data only, data + quotes, or all including photos.
- **You can review materials.** If you provide quotes, we will share the draft with you for approval before publication.

#### Compensation
You will not receive payment for sharing your story. Your participation helps improve care for future mothers.

## Administration Guide

### When to Obtain Consent
- At pilot enrollment (include in consent packet)
- At Week 4 midpoint (invite feedback from engaged patients)
- At Week 6 exit survey (collect from satisfied patients)

### Who Obtains
- Practice staff (preferred — existing patient relationship)
- Firstweeks team member (if facilitating directly)

### Storage
- Signed original: Retain at practice (medical record)
- Scanned copy: Send to Firstweeks (pilot@firstweeks.health)
- Retention: Duration of marketing use + 3 years, per HIPAA

### Quote Approval Workflow

```
Patient provides quote via app or interview
    → Firstweeks drafts marketing material with quote
    → Draft sent to patient for review (email preferred)
    → Patient approves or requests edits
    → Material published with patient's chosen alias
    → Patient notified when material goes live
```
