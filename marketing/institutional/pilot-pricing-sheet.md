## Pilot Partner Pricing (Year 1 — 50% Intro Discount)

| Tier | Standard Annual Price | Pilot Partner Year 1 | What They Get |
|------|---------------------|---------------------|---------------|
| **Basic** | $6,000/yr | $3,000/yr | Quarterly aggregate reports + clinician dashboard + email support |
| **Professional** | $24,000/yr | $12,000/yr | Monthly updates + custom queries + raw cohort exports + phone support |
| **Enterprise** | $60,000/yr | $36,000/yr | Real-time dashboard + API access + dedicated success manager + on-site annual visit |

**Lock-in:** Pilot pricing is locked for 3 years (Years 2-3 at standard rate) if signed by Oct 1, 2026.

---

## What's Included Per Tier

### Basic ($3,000/yr — Year 1)
- Quarterly aggregate outcome report (PDF, 10-15 pages)
- Clinician dashboard access (3 seats)
- Cohort-level trends (sleep, mood, feeding, physical recovery, escalations)
- Email support (2 business day response)
- Data Use Agreement included

### Professional ($12,000/yr — Year 1)
- Everything in Basic, plus:
- Monthly outcome updates (not quarterly)
- Custom cohort queries (up to 4 per year)
- Raw de-identified cohort data export (CSV/JSON)
- Benchmarking against aggregate Firstweeks population
- Phone support (next business day)
- Priority feature requests

### Enterprise ($36,000/yr — Year 1)
- Everything in Professional, plus:
- Real-time clinician dashboard
- API access for EHR integration
- Dedicated success manager
- Annual on-site strategic review
- Custom report builder
- Same-day support
- First access to new data products

---

## Payment Terms

- **Annual upfront:** Full discount applies (as shown above)
- **Quarterly installment:** Add 10% ($3,300/yr / $13,200/yr / $39,600/yr)
- **Monthly:** Not available for Basic; add 15% for Professional/Enterprise
- **Net 30** on invoice
