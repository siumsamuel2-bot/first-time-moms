## 2. Data Use

### 2.1 Data Provided by Firstweeks
- De-identified, aggregate outcome reports derived from Subscriber's patient cohort
- Aggregate benchmarking data from Firstweeks' broader user base (where n ≥ 5)
- Raw de-identified cohort data exports (Professional and Enterprise tiers only)

### 2.2 Permitted Uses
Subscriber may use the data for:
- Internal quality improvement programs
- Clinical research (with separate IRB approval if required)
- Board and payer reporting
- Publication (with Firstweeks co-authorship on data methodology)
- Patient care protocol refinement

### 2.3 Prohibited Uses
Subscriber may NOT:
- Re-identify individual patients from de-identified data
- Sell or sublicense raw data to third parties
- Use data for purposes that violate HIPAA or applicable law
- Publish data without Firstweeks review of methodology and attribution

## 3. De-Identification

Firstweeks warrants that all data provided under this Agreement is de-identified in accordance with HIPAA Safe Harbor method (45 CFR § 164.514(b)):
- No direct identifiers collected or transmitted
- All timestamps shifted by ±72 hours
- Geography at state/region level only
- Minimum cell size of 5 for all cohort queries
- Annual third-party de-identification audit

## 4. Data Ownership

### 4.1 Patient Data
Subscriber retains full ownership of all identifiable patient data. Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of patient medical records or PHI to Firstweeks.

### 4.2 De-Identified Data
De-identified outcome data may be used by Firstweeks in aggregate form for product improvement, research, and marketing, subject to the de-identification standards in Section 3.

### 4.3 Deliverables
Reports, dashboards, and exports provided under this Agreement are licensed to Subscriber for internal use only.

## 5. HIPAA & BAA

A separate Business Associate Agreement (BAA) between Firstweeks and Subscriber governs any PHI access during service delivery. This Agreement incorporates the BAA by reference.

## 6. Term & Termination

### 6.1 Term
Initial term of 1 year from Effective Date. Automatically renews for successive 1-year terms unless either party gives 30 days written notice of non-renewal.

### 6.2 Termination for Cause
Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within 30 days of written notice.

### 6.3 Effect of Termination
Upon termination, Subscriber's access to dashboards and reports will cease. Firstweeks will provide a final data export within 30 days. De-identified aggregate data already published or incorporated into Firstweeks' broader dataset will not be removed.
