# Ambassador Program Guide — Phase 3

**Owner:** CMO / Community Manager
**Timeline:** Weeks 7-12 (Phase 3)
**Prerequisites:** Phase 1 (value posting) + Phase 2 (soft Firstweeks mentions) complete

---

## 1. Ambassador Persona

**Target profile:**
- Active in 3+ parenting communities (Reddit, FB, maybe TikTok/IG)
- 500+ karma in mom subreddits OR 1,000+ members in FB mom group they admin
- Genuine, helpful voice — not a brand promoter
- Postpartum experience within last 2 years
- Already organically recommends products/services in communities

**Red flags:** Overly promotional, uses marketing language, sells MLM products

## 2. Recruitment Strategy

### Source Candidates From

| Source | Method | Expected Yield |
|--------|--------|---------------|
| Phase 1 community interactions | Identify superusers who gave great advice in threads | 20 candidates |
| Reddit PM | "I've seen your comments in r/beyondthebump — you give amazing advice. Would you be interested in a program for voices like yours?" | 10 responses |
| Facebook DM | Similar approach in group context | 5 responses |

### Screening

| Question | Pass Criteria |
|----------|--------------|
| Why do you participate in mom communities? | Genuine desire to help, not personal brand building |
| How would you describe Firstweeks to a friend? | Accurate, warm, non-salesy |
| What would make you excited about this program? | Community impact, not free stuff |
| Sample post: write a discussion prompt for new moms | Authentic, engaging, no corporate language |

**Target:** 10 ambassadors selected from 30-40 candidates.

## 3. Ambassador Program Details

### Perks

| Tier | Criteria | Monthly Perks | Annual Value |
|------|----------|---------------|-------------|
| Core (8) | 1 organic mention/week in relevant threads | Free MammaSync Plus lifetime + $25 gift card | $490 |
| Senior (2) | 2 mentions/week + mentor new ambassadors | Free Plus + $50 gift card + early feature access | $740 |
| Founding (honorary) | First 3 recruited (pilot users) | Free Plus lifetime + $75 gift card + named in "Built with Moms" | $1,140 |

### Responsibilities

**Core Ambassador:**
- 1 genuine recommendation or mention of Firstweeks per week in relevant conversation
- No scripted copy — must be in their own authentic voice
- Participate in monthly 30-min group feedback call
- Flag any product issues or community sentiment to CM

**Senior Ambassador (same as Core +):**
- Mentor 1 new ambassador through first 2 weeks
- 2 mentions/week
- Review 1 piece of content/month for authenticity

### Code of Conduct

1. **Be honest.** Always disclose your connection to Firstweeks when recommending.
2. **Never diagnose.** Flag, suggest, refer — never "you have X."
3. **No pressure.** If someone isn't interested, move on.
4. **Protect privacy.** Never share others' health information.
5. **Be kind.** You represent Firstweeks — warm, non-judgmental, permission-giving.
6. **No scripts.** We trust your voice. Don't use ours.

**Violation:** First warning (verbal). Second: removal from program.

## 4. Ambassador Content Playbook

### Content Types

| Type | Example | Frequency |
|------|---------|-----------|
| Personal experience | "I've been using Firstweeks for a month and here's what surprised me" | 1x/month |
| Thread reply | Someone asks about anxiety tracking → recommend Firstweeks quiz | 2x/month |
| Discussion prompt | "What tools do you use to track your postpartum recovery?" | 1x/month |
| Sharing milestone | Share check-in streak or quiz result (anonymized) | 1x/month |

### Do's and Don'ts for Ambassador Posts

**Do:**
- "I found this app called Firstweeks that's been really helping me track my recovery"
- "My OB recommended I try this and I'm honestly surprised how much it helps"
- "They have a free quiz that tells you what kind of postpartum support you need"

**Don't:**
- "Firstweeks has clinically validated AI-powered symptom tracking"
- "Sign up now using my referral code [link]"
- "This is not medical advice but Firstweeks recommends..."

### Monthly Themes

| Month | Theme | Content Angle |
|-------|-------|---------------|
| Month 1 | Discovery | How I found Firstweeks + what I thought |
| Month 2 | Habit | How tracking changed my daily routine |
| Month 3 | Impact | A specific way it helped me (mood, flag, community) |

## 7. UGC Campaign — #MyFirstweeks

### Campaign Brief

**Goal:** Collect 50+ pieces of user-generated content showing real postpartum moments with Firstweeks.

**Incentive:** Featured on Firstweeks Instagram + 3-month free MammaSync Plus.

**Prompt:** "Share your postpartum reality — the good, the bad, the spit-up."

**Submission format:** Photo or 15-60 second video.

**Content guidelines:**
- Real moments (candid, not staged)
- Optional: show the Firstweeks app in use
- Must be postpartum content (any stage 0-12 weeks)
- Include #MyFirstweeks hashtag
- Tag @firstweeks (IG/TikTok)

### UGC Content Calendar

| Week | Theme | Example Prompt |
|------|-------|----------------|
| 1 | Reality check | "Your 3 AM vs 3 PM side-by-side" |
| 2 | Recovery win | "One thing that got easier this week" |
| 3 | Community | "Your postpartum village" |
| 4 | Check-in | "Show us your check-in moment" |

### Selection Criteria

| Criterion | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| Authenticity (not staged) | 40% |
| Visual quality (well-lit, in focus) | 20% |
| Diversity representation | 20% |
| Brand alignment (warm, real, permission-giving) | 20% |

**Weekly winner:** Featured on Firstweeks IG + 3-month free Plus.
