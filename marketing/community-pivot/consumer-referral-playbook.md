# Consumer Referral Campaign Playbook — Option C

Builds on `marketing/referral-loop-design.md`. This playbook requires no human-touch external outreach — it's designed for AI and digital execution.

---

## Phase 1: Seed (Weeks 1-2)

### Goal
Establish organic presence in 5 key new-mom communities before any Firstweeks mention.

### Target Communities
| Platform | Community | Entry Strategy |
|----------|-----------|----------------|
| Reddit | r/beyondthebump, r/BabyBumps, r/newparents | Value-first posting (no links) |
| Facebook | "May 2026 Babies" / "June 2026 Babies" groups | Join, engage in threads, become trusted member |
| TikTok | #postpartum #fourthtrimester #newmom | Organic educational content |
| Instagram | Momfluencer comment networks | Engage authentically |
| WhatsApp | Existing mom circles (referral-based) | Direct share from early test users |

### Week 1 Posts (Reddit)

**r/beyondthebump — "What I wish someone had told me about week 2"**
- Format: Personal-experience style text post
- Hook: "No one prepares you for the emotional drop after the first week"
- Body: 3 concrete, relatable observations about sleep, feeding guilt, and the "is this normal?" spiral
- No Firstweeks mention. No links. Just genuine community participation.

**r/BabyBumps — "Third trimester prep I actually used postpartum"**
- Format: List post
- Hook: "Everyone tells you to prep for labor. No one tells you to prep for what comes after."
- Body: 5 practical items (meal prep, pelvic floor awareness, mental health check-in habit, etc.)
- No Firstweeks mention. Build credibility.

**r/newparents — "When did you stop Googling symptoms at 2 AM?"**
- Format: Discussion prompt
- Hook: "I'm 3 weeks in and I've probably Googled 'normal newborn breathing' 15 times"
- Body: Start conversation about health anxiety in early parenting
- Identify superusers for future ambassador program

### Week 2: Escalation to Soft Mention

After 7 days of consistent value-first posting, introduce Firstweeks naturally:

**r/beyondthebump — "I found an app that actually helped my anxiety"**
- Format: Personal recommendation post
- Hook: "My OB recommended this app and I'm honestly shocked at how much it helps"
- Body: Firstweeks as a tool for tracking recovery, NOT as a medical device. Frame as "I use it to track my mood and sleep so I have something concrete to tell my doctor"
- Include call-to-action: "They have a free quiz that tells you what kind of support you actually need"
- Link: firstweeks.health/quiz

## Phase 2: Growth (Weeks 3-6)

### Incentive Structure

| Tier | Referrals | Reward | Cost/User |
|------|-----------|--------|-----------|
| 1 | 1 successful trial | 1 free week MammaSync Plus | $5 |
| 2 | 3 successful trials | 1 free month MammaSync Plus | $20 |
| 3 | 5 successful trials | 3 free months + Ambassador badge | $60 |
| 4 | 10+ successful trials | Free lifetime MammaSync Plus | $240 |

Referee gets: 14-day trial (vs standard 7) + personalized onboarding.

### Viral Mechanics

**Post-Quiz Share** — After completing the Personalized Advice Quiz, show share screen:
- "Your results might help a friend going through the same thing"
- Share as image card with personalized result preview (no PHI)
- Channels: WhatsApp, iMessage, Instagram Stories

**Check-In Streak Share** — After 7-day streak, prompt:
- "You've checked in 7 days straight. Share your milestone?"
- Auto-generates shareable: "I've been tracking my recovery for 7 days with Firstweeks"

**"Catch It Early" Card** — If app flags a potential concern AND user confirms they'll follow up with provider:
- "You caught something early. Most new moms miss this."
- Share to warn/help a friend

### Facebook Group Strategy

Target 5 private Facebook groups for "June 2026 Babies":
- Join each group (request membership)
- Engage in existing threads for 3-5 days before posting
- Post discussion prompts:
  1. "What's the one thing you wish your hospital told you before discharge?"
  2. "How are we all sleeping? (Spoiler: probably not great)"
  3. "Has anyone tried a postpartum tracking app? Thoughts?"
- After engagement established, share quiz link in relevant thread context

## Phase 3: Scale (Weeks 7-12)

### Ambassador Program
Recruit top 10 organic community members:
- Criteria: Active in 3+ communities, 500+ karma/karma in mom subreddits, genuine engagement
- Perks: Free MammaSync Plus lifetime, monthly $50 gift card, early feature access
- Responsibilities: 1 organic mention per week in relevant threads, no scripted copy

### UGC Campaign
- Prompt: "Share your postpartum reality — the good, the bad, the spit-up"
- Content: Photo/video of real postpartum moment with #MyFirstweeks
- Incentive: Featured on Firstweeks Instagram + 3-month free subscription
- Distribution: Cross-post winning entries to Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest

### TikTok Script Pipeline

| Week | Format | Topic | Sound |
|------|--------|-------|-------|
| 3 | Talking head | "3 things nobody tells you about the fourth trimester" | Trending audio |
| 4 | Day-in-life | "What my postpartum check-in actually looks like" | Lofi beats |
| 5 | Educational | "Baby blues vs postpartum depression — how to tell" | Soft piano |
| 6 | UGC repost | "Real moms share their recovery wins" | User audio |
| 7 | Product demo | "I use this app to track my recovery. Here's why." | Minimalist |
| 8 | Emotional | "One thing that helped my PPA more than therapy waitlist" | Ambient |
