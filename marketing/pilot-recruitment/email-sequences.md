## Email Sequence Technical Notes

### Send cadence
- Sequence 1: Days 0, 3, 7, 10, 14 (relative to waitlist signup)
- Sequence 2: Days 0 (qualification confirmed), 5 days before deadline, 1 day before start
- Sequence 3: Every Sunday for 8 weeks (2 pre-pilot motivation + 6 during + 1 post)

### Email service provider
- SendGrid recommended (transactional + marketing)
- All emails must pass SPF/DKIM/DMARC

### Tracking
- Open rates target: >45% (healthcare baseline, transactional nature)
- Click rates target: >15%
- Unsubscribe rate target: <0.5%
- Segment by: practice, delivery type, engagement level

### Personalization tokens
- `{{first_name}}`
- `{{practice_name}}`
- `{{onboarding_deadline}}`
- `{{invite_code}}`
- `{{weekly_report_link}}`
- `{{exit_interview_link}}`
- `{{check_in_count}}`

### Compliance
- Every email includes physical address + unsubscribe
- No PHI in email (links to secure app only)
- CAN-SPAM and GDPR compliant
