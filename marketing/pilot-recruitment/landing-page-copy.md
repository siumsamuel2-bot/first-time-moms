## PROBLEM SECTION: "Why This Pilot Matters"

### Headline
"1 in 7 moms experience postpartum complications. Most go undetected until the 6-week check-up — or later."

### Body
Current postpartum care has a critical gap: you go home, and your next clinical touchpoint is 6 weeks later. In those 42 days, complications like postpartum depression, infection, and feeding difficulties can escalate silently.

Firstweeks closes this gap with daily AI-driven check-ins that flag concerns early — and connect you to your care team before things get serious.

### Pilot Goal
Validate that daily digital monitoring + clinician escalation improves:
- Early detection of postpartum depression (EPDS score changes)
- Feeding complication identification
- Physical recovery red-flag detection
- Patient satisfaction and peace of mind

## FAQ

### **Is this really free?**
Yes. No credit card required. You get full MammaSync Plus access for 6 weeks at $0.

### **What data is shared with my doctor?**
Only de-identified risk flags and weekly trend summaries. Your raw check-in responses and personal notes stay private. You control what's shared.

### **What if the app flags something concerning?**
Your OB-GYN's office receives an alert within 24 hours. They'll reach out per their standard protocol. You also get immediate in-app resources and crisis hotlines.

### **What happens after 6 weeks?**
You keep the app. Pilot graduates get 50% off MammaSync Plus for life ($10/mo instead of $20). No pressure to continue.

### **Is this HIPAA-compliant?**
Yes. Firstweeks is built on HIPAA-compliant infrastructure (AWS, encrypted, BAA-signed vendors). Pilot data is de-identified for research; your PHI stays with your practice.

### **Can I withdraw?**
Absolutely. At any time, for any reason. No questions asked. Your data is deleted upon request.
