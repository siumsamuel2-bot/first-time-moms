## 1. Pilot Summary (Quick Reference)

**What:** 8-week pilot of Firstweeks postpartum monitoring app
**Patients:** 30 per practice (60 total)
**Duration:** 8 weeks per patient (weeks 1-8 postpartum)
**Start:** Target Jul 28, 2026
**End:** Sep 11, 2026
**Practice commitment:** 4-6 hours/month from a pilot champion

**Patient flow:**
1. Discharge → text invitation to create account
2. Days 4-42 → twice-weekly AI check-ins (3-5 questions)
3. Threshold crossed → dashboard alert + patient resources
4. Week 6 → discharge + exit survey
5. Ongoing → postpartum library + community available

## 5. Technical Setup Guide

### For the Practice

**Requirements:**
- Internet connection (any)
- Email address for dashboard access
- 15 minutes for initial setup

**Dashboard access:**
1. Firstweeks sends practice admin a dashboard link
2. Admin creates account with work email
3. Dashboard shows: enrolled patients, alert status, weekly trends
4. No software installation needed — browser-based

**Alert notifications:**
- Dashboard badge when patients cross thresholds
- Optional email/SMS for critical alerts
- Practice configures notification preferences

### For Patients

- iOS 15+ or Android 10+
- Download from App Store or Google Play
- No bluetooth or special hardware needed
- Data usage: ~5MB/month

## 6. Practice Champion Role

**Title:** Pilot Champion (existing staff, ~4-6 hrs/month)
**Ideal candidate:** Nurse, midwife, or practice manager

**Responsibilities:**
- Weekly 30-min check-in with Firstweeks CMO
- Identify eligible patients for enrollment
- Distribute enrollment materials
- Address patient questions about the app
- Review dashboard alerts weekly
- Provide feedback at pilot midpoint and end

**Compensation:** $500 honorarium for pilot completion + co-author recognition on any published results

## 9. Success Definition

The pilot is successful when:
1. ≥85% of enrolled patients complete onboarding
2. ≥70% check-in adherence at day 7
3. ≥40% escalation acceptance rate (when flagged)
4. NPS ≥ 50 from exit survey
5. ≥1 complication caught and addressed early
6. Practice expresses interest in continuing post-pilot
