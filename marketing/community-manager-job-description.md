# Community & Content Manager — Job Description

**Company:** Firstweeks Health
**Role:** Community & Content Manager (Part-Time)
**Hours:** 20 hours/week, remote
**Compensation:** $1,500-2,000/month
**Reports to:** CMO
**Start:** Immediate

---

## About Firstweeks

Firstweeks is a personalized postpartum companion app that helps new mothers track their recovery, catch complications early, and connect with a community that gets it. We combine AI-powered daily check-ins with clinical-grade screening — turning the fourth trimester from a blur of worry into a supported, informed journey.

We're pre-launch and looking for a community builder to help us grow authentically in the spaces where new moms already gather.

---

## The Role

You'll be the human voice of Firstweeks in online mom communities. This is not a marketing role in the traditional sense — you won't be running ads or writing press releases. Instead, you'll be a genuine, valuable presence in Reddit parenting subreddits, private Facebook mom groups, and on social platforms where postpartum conversations happen.

Your job is to build trust first, then gently introduce Firstweeks as a resource when it's relevant.

---

## Responsibilities

### Community Engagement (12h/week)
- Join and actively participate in 5+ target Facebook mom groups and 3 Reddit parenting subreddits
- Reply to threads, answer questions, and offer genuine support — no scripts, no sales pitch
- Identify superusers and potential ambassadors for future program
- Escalate crisis concerns (self-harm mentions) per SOP

### Content Creation (5h/week)
- Create 8-10 posts per week across Reddit, Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok
- Adapt existing content (12 blog posts, 4-week content calendar) for each platform's voice
- Create simple graphics in Canva for Instagram and Pinterest
- Film and edit basic TikTok content (phone-quality is fine — authenticity over polish)

### Community Insights (2h/week)
- Log daily observations: what are new moms struggling with today?
- Flag trending topics and concerns to CMO for content planning
- Monitor brand mentions and competitor activity

### Analytics & Reporting (1h/week)
- Track engagement metrics (karma, reactions, shares, link clicks)
- Report weekly to CMO on community health and growth
- Maintain engagement tracker spreadsheet

---

## What We're Looking For

### Must-Have
- Personal experience with the postpartum period (your own, a partner's, or a close family member's)
- Active Reddit account with 500+ karma in parenting subreddits
- Active Facebook account with membership in 3+ mom groups
- Excellent written communication — warm, authentic, non-corporate voice
- Understanding that we never share medical advice or individual health information
- Comfortable on camera (TikTok/Reels) or willing to learn

### Nice-to-Have
- Experience with content scheduling tools (Buffer, Later, Hootsuite)
- Basic design skills (Canva)
- Previous community management or moderation experience
- Understanding of HIPAA basics

### Red Flags (we will not hire)
- Corporate/marketing-speak in writing samples
- Tendency to give unsolicited medical advice
- Unable to separate personal opinion from brand voice
- No demonstrated history of genuine participation in online communities

---

## Voice & Tone

You'll embody the Firstweeks brand voice:
- **Warm, not corporate** — "We" not "Firstweeks officially recommends"
- **Peer, not expert** — "I found this helpful" not "Studies show you should"
- **Permission-giving** — "It's okay if you're not tracking everything"
- **Never diagnose** — Flag, suggest, refer. Never "you have X."
- **Never sell in first touch** — First 5 interactions = value only
