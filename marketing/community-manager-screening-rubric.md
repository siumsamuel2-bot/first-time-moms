# Community Manager — Screening & Interview Rubric

**Role:** Community & Content Manager (Part-Time, 20h/wk, $1,500-2k/mo)
**Hiring Manager:** CMO

---

## Application Screening (Must-Pass Gate)

| Criteria | How to Check | Pass/Fail |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Personal postpartum experience | Note in application or explicitly stated | Must-pass |
| Active Reddit account, 500+ karma in parenting subs | Ask for username or describe activity | Must-pass |
| Active FB account, member of 3+ mom groups | Describe groups and participation | Must-pass |
| Writing sample is warm, authentic, non-corporate | Read sample post for brand voice fit | Must-pass |
| No red-flag language (medical advice, corporate speak) | Scan entire application | Must-pass |

---

## Interview Scorecard (Score 1-5 per dimension)

### 1. Community Instinct
- Understands that community building = value-first, not sales-first
- Can articulate how they'd respond to a mom asking "is this pain normal?"
- Knows when to escalate (self-harm, medical emergency mentions)

### 2. Content Creation
- Can adapt a blog post into a Reddit thread, Instagram Reel, and FB group post
- Understands platform-specific voice (Reddit = conversational, IG = visual, FB = supportive)
- Willing to film phone-quality TikToks

### 3. Brand Voice Alignment
- Writing sample matches Firstweeks voice pillars: clinically kind, calmly confident, permission-giving, straightforward
- Uses "postpartum" not "postnatal", "feeding your baby" not breastfeeding by default
- No scare tactics, no diagnosis claims

### 4. HIPAA & Boundaries Awareness
- Understands they cannot give medical advice
- Knows what constitutes a flag vs. a diagnosis
- Can describe when and how to escalate a crisis

### 5. Cultural Fit
- Genuinely cares about postpartum mental health
- Non-judgmental, inclusive language
- Comfortable with imperfect, real-mom content (stretch marks, tired eyes, crying)

---

## Interview Questions

1. "A mom in our FB group posts: 'I'm 3 weeks postpartum and feel like I'm failing. My friend seems to have it all together.' How do you respond?"
2. "Walk me through how you'd repurpose our blog post 'The First 6 Weeks' into a Reddit post, an Instagram Reel, and a FB group discussion."
3. "A user DMs you saying 'I think I have PPD.' What do you do?"
4. "Show me a post you've written in a mom group that got good engagement. Why did it work?"
5. "What's your schedule like? Can you commit to 20 hours/week, including weekend check-ins?"
