# 90-Day Content Calendar — Firstweeks Marketing

**Objective**: Drive organic traffic, capture leads via the Personalized Advice Quiz, and build community trust before and after public launch.

---

## Channel Strategy

| Channel | Cadence | Content Type | Primary Goal |
|---------|---------|-------------|--------------|
| **Blog** | 2×/week (Tue, Thu) | Long-form SEO content, guides, expert interviews | Organic search traffic |
| **Instagram** | Daily (Mon–Sun) | Stories, carousels, Reels, testimonials | Brand awareness + community |
| **TikTok** | 3×/week | Short-form educational + emotional content | Viral reach + new audience |
| **Pinterest** | 2×/week | Infographics, checklists, quote cards | Evergreen referral traffic |
| **LinkedIn** | 1×/week | Articles, OB-GYN partnerships, company updates | B2B + investor visibility |
| **Email (nurture)** | 2×/week | Newsletter, quiz results, product updates | Lead nurturing + conversion |
| **Community (app)** | Daily | Moderated discussion, AMAs, challenges | Engagement + retention |
| **Reddit / Forums** | 2×/week | Value-first comments in new-mom subreddits | Authentic word-of-mouth |

## Weekly Cadence Template

### Monday — Community & Engagement
- **Instagram Story**: "Monday Mood Check" poll
- **Community**: Introduce weekly theme + discussion prompt
- **Reddit/Forum**: Answering questions in r/beyondthebump, r/Mommit, etc.

### Tuesday — Blog Post #1
- **Blog**: Publish long-form article (SEO optimized)
- **Instagram**: Carousel summarizing key points
- **Email**: Send to list with CTA to read blog and take quiz
- **Pinterest**: Pin the blog graphic

### Wednesday — Midweek Engagement
- **TikTok**: Educational or emotional video related to the theme
- **Instagram**: Reel or carousel teaser of the TikTok video
- **Email (nurture)**: Value-only tip, no hard sell

### Thursday — Blog Post #2
- **Blog**: Publish second article (often a listicle or how-to)
- **Instagram**: Story series with swipe-up (or link in bio) to blog
- **LinkedIn**: Share blog with professional angle (if OB/GYN focused)

### Friday — User-Generated Content & Weekend Prep
- **Instagram**: UGC repost or testimonial
- **TikTok**: Lighter / humorous content
- **Email**: Weekend read recommendation + community invitation
- **Community**: Weekend challenge (e.g., "Share your #MomWin")

### Saturday — Engagement & Growth
- **Instagram**: Interactive content ( LMS, this-or-that)
- **Pinterest**: Pin new blog posts with keyword-rich descriptions

### Sunday — Pre-Week Prep & Reflection
- **Instagram**: Week-ahead preview
- **Community**: Sunday gratitude thread or reflection prompt

## SEO Keyword Strategy

### Primary Keywords (High Volume)
- postpartum recovery
- postpartum depression symptoms
- baby blues vs postpartum depression
- breastfeeding tips for new moms
- newborn sleep schedule
- c-section recovery timeline
- postpartum care plan

### Secondary Keywords (Long-tail)
- how to tell if I have postpartum anxiety
- what to expect first 6 weeks postpartum
- best diet for breastfeeding moms
- how to help a partner with postpartum depression
- newborn feeding every 2 hours
- postpartum exercise when to start

### Question Keywords (Voice Search + Featured Snippets)
- "What are the signs of postpartum depression?"
- "How long does postpartum bleeding last?"
- "Why is my baby crying so much?"
- "When can I exercise after giving birth?"

## Content Repurposing Plan

- **Blog → Carousel**: Key takeaways from each blog post → Instagram carousel
- **Blog → Reel**: Most visually interesting section → 30–60 sec Reel
- **Blog → Infographic**: Data/statistics → Pinterest pin
- **TikTok → Blog**: Expand a viral TikTok concept into a full blog post
- **Community Q&A → Blog**: Common questions from the app community → FAQ blog post
- **Quiz Results → Testimonial**: Anonymized quotes from quiz users → Social proof content
