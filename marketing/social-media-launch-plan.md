## Content Pillars

### 1. Education (40%)
Evidence-based postpartum information that builds credibility and SEO traffic.
- Recovery timelines and milestones
- Warning signs and when to seek help
- Feeding, sleep, and mental health guides
- Partner/family support resources

### 2. Emotional Connection (30%)
Stories and content that make mothers feel seen, validated, and less alone.
- "No one told me" posts
- Honest postpartum realities
- Community spotlights and UGC
- Self-compassion and permission-giving content

### 3. Product / Lead Magent (20%)
Drives quiz completions, app downloads, and trial signups.
- Quiz result previews
- Feature highlights and benefits
- Comparison content (with vs. without Firstweeks)
- Case studies and testimonials

### 4. Community & Interaction (10%)
Engagement-driven content that builds two-way connection.
- Polls and questions
- Challenges and prompts
- AMAs and expert Q&As
- User-generated content reposts

## Brand Voice Guidelines

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Tone | Warm, authoritative, non-judgmental |
| Language | Clinical accuracy without jargon |
| Empathy | "We know this is hard" — always validate before educating |
| Urgency | Soft urgency for health, never fear-mongering |
| Pronouns | You (mother), we (Firstweeks), she (when referencing a user) |
| Values | Evidence-based, inclusive, respectful of all feeding choices, body-positive |

**Do:**
- "Your body is doing something extraordinary. Here's how to support it."
- "Most complications are treatable when caught early. Here's what to watch for."
- "Every feeding journey is valid. Fed is what matters."

**Don't:**
- "You should be sleeping when the baby sleeps." (dismissive)
- "Breast is best." (exclusive, shaming)
- "Don't worry, it gets better!" (invalidating current struggle)
