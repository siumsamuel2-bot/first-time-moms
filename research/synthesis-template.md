## Quality Check

Before finalizing synthesis:

- [ ] Every participant has a completed summary sheet

- [ ] Themes are backed by direct quotes, not interpretation alone
- [ ] Delivery type analysis is complete
- [ ] Mental health history sub-analysis is complete
- [ ] Design implications are actionable (not abstract)
- [ ] Quotes are anonymized (remove identifying details)
- [ ] Priority ratings reflect engineering feasibility + user impact
