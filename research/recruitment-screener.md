# Target User Criteria & Recruitment Screener

**FIR-81** | UX Designer | July 2026

---

## Target User Profile

### Must-Have Criteria

| Criteria | Detail |
|---|---|
| First-time mother | Yes — no previous live births |
| Postpartum window | 0-6 weeks at time of interview |
| Age | 22-40 |
| English proficient | Conversational + written |
| US-based | For product-market fit and provider network relevance |
| Smartphone owner | Uses apps regularly |

### Stratification Targets (aim for diversity across)

| Dimension | Target Mix | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery type | 50% vaginal, 30% scheduled C-section, 20% emergency C-section | Recovery profiles differ significantly; UX must adapt per type |
| Feeding method | Mixed: EBF, EFF, combination | Feeding challenges are a major source of anxiety |
| Mental health history | Include 1-2 participants with prior anxiety/depression history | Critical for understanding sensitivity to escalation UX |
| Partner status | Mix of partnered and solo | Support network access varies |
| Geographic region | At least 3 states (ideally >2 time zones) | Regional differences in postpartum care access |
| Race / ethnicity | Reflect US demographic diversity | Avoid designing for a single demographic |
| Income / insurance | Mix of private + public (Medicaid) | Access to care differs; app must serve all |
| Urban / suburban / rural | Include at least 1 rural participant | Provider availability is a key UX constraint |

### Nice-to-Have Criteria

- Has used a health or wellness app in the past 6 months
- Experienced a NICU stay (even brief)
- Identifies as LGBTQ+ (for inclusive design validation)
- Non-English primary language (for future localization roadmap)

### Exclusion Criteria

- Currently hospitalized for psychiatric reasons
- Involved in a postpartum psychosis episode (per their own report)
- Works in UX research, product design, or maternal healthcare (domain expertise bias)

## Recruitment Pipeline

| Source | Owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Pilot OB-GYN practices | CMO | In-clinic flyers with QR code |
| Postpartum support groups | CMO | Online groups (Facebook, PSI) |
| Social media (Instagram, TikTok) | CMO | Targeted ads → screener link |
| Mom forums (Reddit r/beyondthebump, r/newparents) | CMO | Mod-approved recruitment posts |
| Referral from enrolled participants | UX Designer | Snowball sampling (up to 2 per participant) |

### Target Recruit Count

| Phase | Target | Buffer (20% no-show) | Screened Needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 (this study) | 8-10 interviews | 10-12 confirmed | ~30-40 screener completes |

## Scheduling & Incentives

- **Incentive:** $50 gift card (Amazon, Target, or Visa) per completed interview
- **Scheduling:** Allow evening and weekend slots (parents of newborns have limited daytime availability)
- **Rescheduling:** Up to 2 reschedules allowed; no-show after that is replaced
- **Reminder cadence:** 48h before → 24h before → 2h before (text preferred over email)
