# Interview Guide — User Research for Firstweeks

**FIR-81** | UX Designer | July 2026

**Format:** Semi-structured remote interview (45 min)
**Participants:** First-time mothers, 0-6 weeks postpartum
**Purpose:** Understand needs, fears, daily workflows, and pain points with current postpartum support

---

## Pre-Interview Setup

- Confirm participant is in a comfortable, private space
- Remind: "There are no right or wrong answers — your honest experience helps us build something better"
- Confirm permission to record (audio + screen)
- Have crisis resource card ready to share post-interview

---

## Interview Questions (17 questions)

### Warm-Up & Rapport (3 min)

1. Tell me a little about your birth story — how did things go? (Broad, non-leading opener that invites storytelling. Notes delivery type, NICU stay, complications.)

2. What has the first few weeks at home been like for you? What's been the biggest surprise?

### Daily Life & Routines (4 min)

3. Walk me through a typical 24-hour day right now. What does your rhythm look like?

4. What tools or apps are you using to track anything — feeding, sleep, mood, recovery? What do you like or dislike about them?

5. When do you usually have a moment to yourself? What does "me time" look like right now?

### Physical Recovery (5 min)

6. How is your physical recovery going? What feels better than expected? What feels harder?

7. (If vaginal delivery) How are you managing perineal tear / episiotomy recovery? What helped? What didn't?

8. (If C-section) How is your incision healing? What has been surprising about C-section recovery that nobody warned you about?

9. What physical symptom caught you off guard that you wish someone had prepared you for?

### Emotional & Mental Health (6 min)

10. How would you describe your emotional state since coming home from the hospital? Has it changed day to day?

11. What does a "good day" look like emotionally? What does a "hard day" look like?

12. Have you ever felt like you should be handling things better, or felt guilty about struggling? How do you talk to yourself about that?

13. Who do you talk to when you're feeling overwhelmed? (Partner, family, friends, provider, online community, no one)

14. Have you been asked about your mental health by any healthcare provider since delivery? What was that experience like? Did it feel judgmental or supportive?

### Support & Information Needs (5 min)

15. Where do you go for answers to your questions about the baby's health or your own recovery? (Google, pediatrician, mom forums, books, etc.) How reliable does that feel?

16. What's the most confusing or contradictory advice you've received since becoming a mom?

17. If an app could help you feel more supported in these first weeks, what would it absolutely need to do — and what would make you delete it immediately?

### Closing (2 min)

18. Is there anything we haven't talked about that you think is important for us to know?

19. What advice would you give to a friend about to have her first baby?

20. Do you have any questions for me?
